TRACK 02

Merchant Risk Engine

Post-Onboarding Fraud Detection & Mitigation

An AI-driven solution to identify chargebacks, analyze risk with SHAP explainability, and automate merchant communication.

THE PROBLEM

The False-Positive Dilemma

Payment aggregators face a constant balancing act between preventing fraud and ruining the merchant experience.

Missed Fraud Costs

  • Direct financial loss via chargebacks
  • Network penalties from Visa/Mastercard
  • Reputational damage to the aggregator

Customer Friction

  • Payout holds on legitimate merchants
  • Increased manual review costs ($50+ per case)
  • Merchant churn due to poor experience
SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

End-to-End MLOps Stack

Built for scale, explainability, and rapid human-in-the-loop processing.

1. Machine Learning Core

Ensemble of XGBoost (70%) and LightGBM (30%) trained on 590K IEEE-CIS transactions.

2. AI Automation Layer

Gemini API interprets high-risk factors and automatically drafts contextual payout hold notices.

3. Modern Infrastructure

Asynchronous FastAPI backend and reactive React/Vite frontend, containerized with Docker.

4. Total Explainability

Native SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) values to justify every risk score mathematically.

PERFORMANCE METRICS

Model Precision & Recall

By tuning the threshold, we achieved 94.2% precision on high-risk flags.
